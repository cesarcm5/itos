Logo

Logo usage

A geometric "I" plus three ascending bars. The growth element is the only violet part. The logotype is "ITOS" in caps, Space Grotesk 600, with wide tracking.

ITOS
ITOS
VariantUseMinimum
Horizontal lockupHeader, landing, emails96 px wide
Mark onlyFavicon, app tile, mobile header16 px
ReversedWhite on ocean-600 or ocean-90096 px wide

Do

  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the arrow on the "I"
  • Use the reversed version on dark sections
  • Keep violet on the growth element only

Don't

  • Recolor outside the palette or make the whole mark violet
  • Add shadows, glow, rotation or stretching
  • Place it on photos or busy backgrounds

Voice

Voice and tone

The UI is in Spanish, written for someone investing for the first time.

Clear

Plain words, and explain each term.

Not rendimiento ajustado por volatilidad

Calm

Steady, no urgency.

Not ¡No te lo pierdas!

Encouraging

Small steps count.

Never patronizing

Honest

Past performance isn't a promise.

Not rendimiento garantizado

Puedes invertir $4,000.00 MXN al mes sin tocar tu fondo de emergencia.
Si hubieras invertido $2,000 al mes en VOO desde 2021, hoy tendrías $X.
No pudimos cargar los precios. Reintentar

Always include on dashboard, simulator and stock pages

"El rendimiento pasado no garantiza resultados futuros. Esto no es asesoría financiera."

Avoid: garantizado, sin riesgo, hazte rico, la mejor inversión, emojis in financial figures.

Minimalist flat vector for "ITOS", a fintech app for first-time investors. Geometric construction, generous white space, calm and trustworthy tech aesthetic. Palette: ocean blue #0B3394 (dominant), violet #6322E0 (single accent marking growth), white #FFFFFF background. No 3D, no shadows, no glow, no gradients except a subtle ocean-to-violet on growth elements, no coins, no dollar signs, no bulls or bears, no text unless specified.
